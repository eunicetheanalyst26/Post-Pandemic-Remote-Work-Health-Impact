# Post-Pandemic Remote Work Health Impact

Power BI report analyzing post-pandemic remote work health impacts based on the June 2025 survey across 3,035 workers.

## Project Objective
Evaluate how post-pandemic work arrangements (**Remote**, **Hybrid**, and **Onsite**) affect weekly working hours, work-life balance, social isolation, burnout rates, and overall employee well-being.

## Tools & Features
* **Power BI Desktop** – Data modeling, DAX measures, and multi-page interactive dashboard design
* **Power Query** – Data cleaning, transformation, and preparation
* **Interactive Slicers & Navigation** – Dynamic filtering by **Work Arrangement** (`Hybrid`, `Onsite`, `Remote`) and **Gender** (`Female`, `Male`), plus page navigation between the **Dashboard** and **Insights and Recommendation** views

## Key Metrics & KPIs
* **Total Number of Workers:** 3.035K (3,035 respondents)
* **Average Work-Life Balance Score:** 2.99 / 5
* **Average Social Isolation Score:** 2.71
* **Average Weekly Hours:** 49.89 hours
* **Overall Burnout Rate:** 33.11%

## Key Insights
* **Long working hours across all groups:** Workers average **49.89 hours per week**—nearly 10 hours above a standard 40-hour week—with every work arrangement falling within ~0.1 hour of that average.
* **Middling work-life balance everywhere:** Scores sit right around **3.0 out of 5** across all groups (**Onsite:** 3.02, **Remote:** 3.00, **Hybrid:** 2.95).
* **Highest burnout and isolation among remote workers:** Remote workers experience the highest burnout share (**42.72%**, vs. **32.96%** Hybrid and **24.31%** Onsite) and the highest social isolation score (**3.5**, vs. **2.8** Hybrid and **2.4** Onsite—roughly 45% higher than Onsite).
* **Burnout by gender:** Among affected workers, burnout splits at **51.82% Female** and **48.18% Male**.
* **Widespread mental health conditions:** Roughly **3 in 4 workers** report a mental health condition across every work arrangement, with only about a quarter reporting none.

## Strategic Recommendations
* **Set limits on working hours:** Introduce workload caps and a clear right-to-disconnect policy to address excessive weekly hours across all work arrangements.
* **Pair hybrid work with clear boundaries:** Do not rely on hybrid schedules alone to improve work-life balance; support teams with structured flexibility and workload boundaries.
* **Expand mental health support universally:** Make mental health resources accessible to all employees rather than targeting a single work arrangement.
* **Strengthen connection for remote workers:** Schedule regular in-person days, team events, and virtual coworking sessions to reduce social isolation.
